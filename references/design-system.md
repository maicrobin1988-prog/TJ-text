# Design System Reference

Use this when matching the company's common interior scheme text-book language, based on the 64-page A3 landscape Zhiyuan B Zone confirmation PDF.

## Format

- Canvas: A3 landscape, 420mm x 297mm. PDF source is 1190.55 x 841.89 pt.
- PPTX adaptation: 16:9 widescreen, preserving generous margins and sparse composition.
- Safe margins: 28-36mm left/right on cover and chapter pages; 18-24mm on image-heavy pages.
- Background: warm white or very pale cool gray. Avoid dark full-deck themes except optimization comparison pages.

## Visual DNA

- Primary atmosphere: clean technology office, white light, cool blue/violet haze, high-key renderings.
- Signature motif: soft blurred arc or light wash in blue, cyan, and lavender, usually occupying the right or lower-right quadrant on cover/chapter pages.
- Secondary accent: small blue/cyan geometric blocks, arrows, route lines, or translucent zone fills.
- Brand restraint: logo is small; the project name and spatial narrative carry the page.
- Image strategy: use real renderings, floor plans, axonometric diagrams, and material callout images. Avoid decorative stock images.

## Color Tokens

- Paper: `#f8faf9`, `#ffffff`
- Ink: `#111111`
- Secondary text: `#7d7d7d`
- Fine line: `#d7dbe2`
- Cool blue: `#87c8e8`
- Electric blue: `#315cff`
- Violet: `#8e7ae6`
- Pale lavender: `#ded6ff`
- Pale cyan: `#dff7fb`
- Pale yellow: `#f6eac8`
- Gray zone: `#cfcfcf`
- Warm material accent: `#d7b77a`, `#c98f38`

## Typography

- Use a thin modern sans-serif look.
- Chinese title: large, light-to-regular weight, black. Avoid heavy decorative fonts except the closing `谢谢`.
- English labels: uppercase or title case, light weight, expanded by whitespace rather than bold weight.
- Bilingual rule: English label first for technical category headers, Chinese after slash or below it. Example: `RENDERING / 电梯厅效果图`.
- Use small gray English subtitles under Chinese item names in diagrams.
- Do not write long body paragraphs on visual pages.

## Header System

- Top-right page header is the default for content slides: `CATEGORY / 中文页名`.
- Top-left small plan locator appears on rendering pages when useful.
- Fine horizontal rules are used sparingly under cover title blocks and in closing pages.
- Page numbers are not visually dominant in the source deck; rely on chapter numbering instead.

## Page Families

1. Cover: huge English title block at left, Chinese project name below, thin divider line, date/logo at lower left, large soft gradient occupying right/lower field.
2. Minimal TOC: almost empty page with small centered/right-aligned chapter entries.
3. Mood/reference collage: black or white background, grid of precedent/rendering images, tiny labels.
4. Full-bleed concept rendering: one cinematic image, often with translucent text overlay.
5. Chapter divider: large two-digit number, English title, Chinese title, short list of chapter subtopics, soft gradient field.
6. Axonometric/function diagram: large gray 3D massing or exploded floor stack, colored translucent highlights, thin leader lines and bilingual labels.
7. Plan analysis: large plan on left/center, right-side title and legend, pale fills for functions or routes.
8. Journey/experience map: large curve, circular image nodes, bilingual labels, dashed vertical guides, low text density.
9. Rendering page: wide rendering occupying 75-85% of page, white top band, top-right category label, optional mini-plan locator.
10. Multi-image rendering index: plan center or left, small renderings around it with dotted red/blue callout boxes.
11. Material page: six image tiles, white pill callouts with leader lines, concise material summary at left.
12. Communication optimization page: dark gray/black background, before-after renderings with gray annotation blocks.
13. Closing page: sparse white field, large gray `THANK YOU`, heavy black `谢谢`, project info and logo at right.
14. Strategy content page: large Chinese thesis, small English category label, and one of several structures: statement with proof points, metric row, 2x2 matrix, split explanation, or timeline.

## Composition Rules

- Keep strong asymmetry. Most pages have a heavy image/diagram area and a quiet label zone.
- On text-heavy strategy pages, do not make all pages share the same two-column composition. Rotate statement, metric, matrix, split, and timeline structures while preserving the same typography, margins, and cool accent system.
- The first page/cover must carry the main project subject clearly: large Chinese title, concise subtitle, and 2-3 short strategic points. Do not let the title become a small label.
- Do not center everything. Use large empty fields.
- Let renderings breathe; avoid explanatory boxes on top unless the page is explicitly a material/callout page.
- Use rounded labels only for small material tags, not for general UI-like cards.
- Use thin leader lines and small dots for callouts.
- If using dark pages, reserve them for optimization/comparison or final review notes, not the main scheme narrative.

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

- Marketing hero style with slogans and button-like elements.
- Dense consulting slide layouts with many cards.
- Pure Chinese report pages without the English technical label layer.
- Saturated gradients, purple-heavy backgrounds, or decorative blobs.
- Overly thick icons, heavy borders, shadows, and boxed paragraphs.
